Practice Area 02

Board Governance & Management

The effectiveness of the Board of Directors directly determines an organization's strategic trajectory and fiduciary security. CORVANTIS advises chairpersons, non-executive directors, and board committees on creating balanced, high-functioning boardroom environments.

1. Board Structure Advisory

Advising on board composition, independence criteria, committee configurations (Audit, Risk, Remuneration, Nominations), and skills matrices.

2. Board Meeting Governance

Structuring board agendas, optimizing board pack delivery, enforcing resolution documentation standards, and minute-taking integrity.

3. Director Responsibilities

Clarifying statutory director duties (duty of care, duty of loyalty), mitigating personal liability, and delivering tailored director onboarding programs.

4. Board Evaluation

Conducting objective, confidential board performance assessments covering individual directors, committee efficacy, and board chair leadership.

5. Corporate Decision-Making

Establishing defensible protocols for major corporate transactions, conflict adjudication, and stakeholder engagement records.

Practice Area 03

Regulatory Compliance Advisory

Modern enterprises operate within overlapping statutory regimes. CORVANTIS designs proactive compliance frameworks that protect entities from regulatory penalties, statutory breach notices, and reputational contagion.

1. Regulatory Compliance Reviews

Conducting detailed baseline audits of statutory filings, licensing prerequisites, and regulator reporting timetables across active markets.

2. Compliance Frameworks

Building operational Compliance Management Systems (CMS) benchmarked against ISO 37301 standards to formalize compliance accountability.

3. Statutory Compliance Guidance

Continuous guidance on local company laws, securities filings, corporate taxation disclosures, and specialized industry licensing rules.

4. Regulatory Risk Identification

Systematic hazard mapping to identify high-risk business activities subject to scrutiny by central banks, trade commissions, or tax authorities.

5. Compliance Monitoring

Instituting ongoing monitoring dashboards, periodic spot-audits, and automated regulatory calendar trackers for compliance personnel.

Practice Area 04

Audit Preparation & Readiness

External statutory audits are high-stakes examinations that consume vast corporate resources when unprepared. CORVANTIS bridges the gap between client finance teams and external auditors, ensuring seamless, query-free audit engagements.

1. Audit Documentation

Standardizing accounting position papers, revenue recognition schedules, related-party disclosure files, and balance sheet substantiations.

2. Internal Controls Assessment

Testing internal financial controls (IFC/ICFR) to detect design deficiencies or operating failures prior to external auditor walkthroughs.

3. Audit Readiness Reviews

Simulated pre-audit reviews identifying documentation gaps, reconciliations backlog, and potential management letter points.

4. Evidence Organization

Building structured, indexed electronic data rooms (PBC schedules) with tamper-evident evidentiary trails for rapid external auditor access.

5. Audit Coordination

Acting as technical advisors alongside the finance leadership, facilitating constructive technical dialogue with external audit partners.

Practice Area 05

Internal Audit & Risk Management

Independent internal assurance gives executive leadership the empirical clarity needed to navigate enterprise vulnerabilities. We design and execute internal audit plans and enterprise risk management (ERM) strategies.

1. Internal Audit Support

Co-sourcing or outsourcing risk-based internal audit functions, delivering objective reports directly to the Audit Committee.

2. Risk Assessment

Designing dynamic risk registers, scoring impact and likelihood parameters, and formulating enterprise risk appetite statements.

3. Internal Control Reviews

Benchmarking internal controls against COSO frameworks to safeguard corporate assets and minimize operational fraud opportunities.

4. Risk Mitigation Planning

Formulating tangible corrective action plans with assigned risk owners and measurable remediation timelines.

5. Operational Compliance

Reviewing procurement integrity, third-party vendor risks, supply chain dependencies, and operational business continuity plans.
