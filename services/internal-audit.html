Overview & Fiduciary Rationale

Modernizing Internal Audit From Compliance Policing to Strategic Catalyst

Internal audit is the essential third line of defense. When executed with strategic depth and analytical rigor, it provides boards with independent truth and operational clarity.

In today’s fast-moving corporate environment, a traditional tick-the-box internal audit department is obsolete. Audit committees require forward-looking assurance that evaluates digital transformation risks, cybersecurity vulnerabilities, supply chain dependencies, and culture, alongside financial controls.

CORVANTIS provides comprehensive Internal Audit services, operating as an independent outsourced department or augmenting existing internal teams through specialized co-sourcing. Built strictly upon the Institute of Internal Auditors (IIA) International Professional Practices Framework, our reviews identify control breakdowns, root causes, and practical, value-generating remedies.

Core Advisory Commitments

  • Risk-Based Annual Audit Planning

  • COSO Internal Control Evaluations

  • Forensic & Fraud Risk Assessments

Vulnerabilities & Roadblocks

Internal Audit Roadblocks That Diminish Value

Organizations with underdeveloped internal audit functions often suffer from blind spots that leave boards exposed to surprise crises.

01

Static Audit Scopes

Conducting the same standardized audits year after year while ignoring emerging digital, operational, and cyber threats.

02

Lack of Specialized Expertise

Internal teams lacking specialized credentials to effectively audit complex cloud IT, treasury algorithms, or AI tools.

03

Adversarial Relationships

Auditors viewed as internal police rather than trusted advisors, leading to operational friction and concealed information.

04

Voluminous, Unactionable Reports

Lengthy audit reports focused on minor administrative infractions while burying critical strategic risks.

05

Delayed Remediation Tracking

Lack of a systematic process to verify whether management has actually remediated past audit findings.

06

Impaired Independence

Internal auditors reporting administratively to executives whose departments they are tasked with evaluating.

Systematic Methodology

The CORVANTIS Risk-Based Internal Audit Lifecycle

A disciplined, IIA-aligned five-stage audit methodology delivering objective assurance and actionable operational value.

Phase 01

Annual Risk Assessment & Charter

Comprehensive consultation with board audit committee and executive leadership to define the dynamic audit universe.

Deliverable: Annual Risk-Based Internal Audit Plan
Phase 02

Engagement Scoping & Planning

Pre-audit walkthroughs, process mapping, and risk-and-control matrix (RCM) development for the targeted operational area.

Deliverable: Engagement Scope & Audit Program
Phase 03

Fieldwork & Empirical Testing

Rigorous sample testing, data analytics queries, and operational interviews evaluating control design and operating efficacy.

Deliverable: Validated Audit Observations & Workpapers
Phase 04

Root-Cause Reporting & Action Plans

Clear reporting detailing issues, business implications, root causes, and agreed-upon management remediation plans.

Deliverable: Final Internal Audit Executive Report
