Illustrative Case 01

Cross-Border Holding Restructuring & Board Accountability

Industry Focus Banking & Multi-Jurisdictional Financial Services

The Business Challenge

A multi-entity financial group operating across three regulatory zones experienced significant friction: subsidiary boards operated with conflicting mandates, executive delegation thresholds were inconsistent, and the holding company board received critical risk reports weeks after internal audit identification.

Advisory Approach

CORVANTIS conducted a 90-day diagnostic review encompassing board charters, committee terms of reference, and subsidiary delegation protocols. We performed structured interviews with 18 directors and benchmarked practices against Basel Committee Corporate Governance Principles.

Proposed Solution

  • Formulated a unified Group Corporate Governance Charter harmonizing subsidiary oversight.
  • Standardized Delegation of Authority (DoA) with real-time escalation triggers.
  • Restructured Audit and Risk Committee composition to satisfy statutory independence thresholds.

Illustrative Outcome

The restructured governance model eliminated committee redundancies across subsidiaries, reduced board reporting lag from 28 days to under 48 hours, and successfully cleared an annual central bank supervisory inspection with zero corporate governance observations.

Result Metric: 100% Board Alignment & Zero Regulatory Deficiencies
Illustrative Case 02

Enterprise Statutory Compliance Framework Implementation

Industry Focus Healthcare & Life Sciences Network

Compliance Challenges

Following rapid acquisitions, a regional healthcare organization operated with decentralized compliance protocols. Multiple facilities missed mandatory statutory reporting updates, leading to warning citations from health regulatory authorities and heightened liability risk.

Assessment Process

CORVANTIS mapped over 140 applicable statutory regulations across corporate filings, clinical licensing, patient data privacy, and biohazard protocols. We identified 38 unassigned compliance obligations and established an enterprise risk heat map.

Proposed Framework

  • Instituted a centralized Compliance Management System (CMS) aligned with ISO 37301.
  • Automated recurring statutory deadline notifications across all regional operating hubs.
  • Established an Executive Compliance Committee with monthly board reporting mandates.

Illustrative Outcome

The healthcare network achieved 100% on-time statutory filing compliance over the subsequent 18 months, remediated all outstanding regulatory citations, and successfully expanded into two new jurisdictions without administrative friction.

Result Metric: Zero Overdue Filings & Full Licensing Reinstatement
Illustrative Case 03

Statutory Year-End Audit Readiness & Internal Controls Overhaul

Industry Focus Advanced Industrial Manufacturing

Business Problem

Prior annual external audits resulted in repeated management letter qualifications, prolonged auditor testing cycles, and mounting professional fee overruns due to disorganized financial schedules and uncoordinated internal evidence files.

Documentation Gaps

Review uncovered significant weaknesses: undocumented journal voucher approvals, inventory reconciliation backlogs across 4 manufacturing sites, missing lease contract modifications, and ad-hoc related-party transactions lacking formal board minutes.

Strategy & Implementation

  • Structured a pre-audit Prepared-By-Client (PBC) digital data room with strict evidence indexing.
  • Pre-cleared technical accounting positions on revenue recognition and lease liabilities.
  • Deployed an interim audit coordination team to handle daily external auditor queries.

Illustrative Outcome

The enterprise completed its statutory year-end audit 3 weeks ahead of regulatory filing deadlines. For the first time in five years, the external auditors issued an unqualified audit opinion with zero material control deficiencies noted in the management letter.

Result Metric: Unqualified Clean Opinion & 40% Reduction in Audit Duration
